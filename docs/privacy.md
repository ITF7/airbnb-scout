# Scout for Airbnb: privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Scout is a browser extension that helps you search Airbnb by describing your trip in plain words. It is free, it has no accounts, and it has no servers of its own. Scout is not affiliated with Airbnb.

## What Scout does with your information

- **Your search request** (for example "London, 28 Nov – 3 Dec, 2 guests, under $1,000") stays in your browser. It is read on your own computer, either by Chrome's built-in AI (if you turn it on) or by simple rules. It is never sent to the developer or to any AI company.
- **Airbnb pages.** When you press Search, Scout opens Airbnb in a tab in your browser and reads the search results and the few listings you ask it to check, the same pages you could open yourself. Scout only reads public listing information: price, beds, description, house rules, check-in details and reviews.
- **No personal data is collected.** Scout removes reviewers' and hosts' names, home towns and profile details from the page before using the text. It never reads your Airbnb account, messages, bookings or payment details, never signs in, and never books anything.
- **Your results** (the shortlist and the report) are saved only in your browser's extension storage, so you can reopen a report. Uninstalling Scout deletes them.

## Services Scout contacts

Only to answer your search, directly from your browser:

| Service | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| airbnb.com | The search you set up (place, dates, guests, price limit) | To show listings |
| data.police.uk (UK only) | The location of a tube station near a listing | Street-crime level around the station |
| api.tfl.gov.uk (London only) | Your arrival point and time, and a station's location | Public-transport route from your arrival point |
| nominatim.openstreetmap.org | The city name | The city-centre location, for "how central" |

None of these receive anything that identifies you beyond what any web request does (such as your IP address), and Scout adds no tracking, analytics or advertising.
